A doctrine, not a marketplace.

niogod™ is not a staffing platform. It is a managed engineering institution — Stuttgart-anchored, Werkvertrag-led, AÜG-konform — that deploys sealed three-role pods into DACH deep-tech mandates.

The doctrine.

// Three principles, declared and contractually enforced

niogod™ exists to translate the operational rigor of a German engineering institution into a remote-delivery architecture that the DACH Mittelstand and its deep-tech successors can underwrite without compliance reservation. The thesis is narrow and declarative: a sealed three-role pod, governed by a single Projektleiter under Werkvertrag, anchored in Stuttgart, executes deep-tech mandates with the legal posture of an in-house team and the cost structure of a remote one. Three principles are non-negotiable.

// Institutional Rigor

Institutionelle Rigorosität.

Every engagement is documented, audit-ready, and structured to survive third-party legal review by a Steuerberater or Fachanwalt für Arbeitsrecht. No verbal arrangements. No ambiguous reporting lines. No retrofitted contracts.

// Contractual Clarity

Vertragliche Klarheit.

Werkvertrag under BGB §631, not Dienstvertrag. Outcome owed, not effort. The Mandant issues no task-level Weisungen to offshore engineers; the Projektleiter is the sole instructional interface. This is the legal trip-wire of verdeckte AÜG — and niogod™'s architecture is built around it.

// Engineering Excellence

Engineering-Exzellenz.

Pilot Engineers are drawn from Tier-1 institutional alumni networks across India — IIT, BITS, IIIT, NIT, ISI, IISc — and pre-filtered through a five-stage vetting funnel with sub-1% acceptance. Senior Consultants carry 10+ years of vertical seniority. The pod is not "vetted contractors"; it is a professional engineering unit.
