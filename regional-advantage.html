One unit. Three roles.
One Werkvertrag.

The niogod™ pod is not a personnel selection — it is a sealed delivery architecture, governed under BGB §631, anchored in Stuttgart, accountable through a single Projektleiter.

Anatomy of the pod.

// Three roles · one Werkvertragsobjekt · one accountable interface

// Projektleiter — DACH Liaison

The single point of contact.

First and only interface to the Geschäftsführung des Mandanten. C1 German native or near-native. Owns sprint planning, risk reporting, escalation paths, and the legal interface between niogod™ and the Mandanten-Gesellschaft. Absorbs the operational complexity of the offshore layer.

// Qualifications

  • C1 German, working English at C1+
  • 5+ years in DACH engineering programme management
  • Experience with deutsches Werkvertragsrecht and AÜG posture

// Operational mandate

  • Sole instructional interface to the offshore layer
  • Weekly Statusbericht to the Mandant CTO
  • Owns Werkvertrag Abnahme criteria
// Senior Consultant — Architecture layer

10+ years of vertical seniority.

Architecture, technical reviews, and the bridge between the Mandant CTO and the Pilot Engineer. Selected per vertical: Robotics, ML/AI, AUTOSAR, SAP S/4HANA, IEC 62304-grade medtech, dual-use defense stacks.

// Qualifications

  • 10+ years in production deep-tech systems within the assigned vertical
  • Tier-1 institutional alumnus or equivalent
  • Track record of architecture ownership at scale

// Operational mandate

  • Architecture and design ownership within the Werk
  • Direct technical supervision of the Pilot Engineer
  • Quarterly architecture review with the Mandant CTO
// Pilot Engineer — Execution layer

The execution layer.

Highly specialized engineer drawn from India's Tier-1 institutional alumni network — IIT, BITS, IIIT, NIT, ISI, IISc. Pre-filtered through the five-stage niogod™ vetting funnel. Operates exclusively under direction of the Senior Consultant, under Werkvertragsmandate of the Projektleiter.

// Qualifications

  • Tier-1 alumnus, 3–15 years production experience
  • Written English at C1+; working German at A2+ a strong plus
  • Five-stage vetting passed (sub-1% acceptance)

// Operational mandate

  • Implementation under Senior Consultant supervision
  • Daily async status to the Projektleiter
  • No direct task-level Weisungen from the Mandant

Why three, not one.

// The standard objection — addressed

Cognitive load distribution. A single freelance senior engineer absorbs PM responsibilities, architecture decisions, and execution simultaneously. Each of those layers degrades under load. The pod separates them: the Projektleiter owns programme management, the Senior Consultant owns architecture, the Pilot Engineer owns execution. The same fully-loaded budget yields three differentiated outputs rather than one diluted one.

Compliance posture. A Werkvertrag under BGB §631 requires an identifiable, result-owing structure with defined Abnahme criteria and a hierarchical instruction chain that does not reach the Mandant. A single freelancer cannot, by structure, support that posture — the Mandant inevitably issues task-level Weisungen, which is the legal trip-wire for verdeckte Arbeitnehmerüberlassung. The three-role pod institutionalizes the protective hierarchy.

Continuity. A single contractor is a single point of failure. Illness, attrition, or scope drift collapses the engagement. The pod, with overlapping seniority and a documented internal handover protocol, sustains delivery across personal disruptions. The Mandant contracts with the institution, not the individual.

Long-cycle deep-tech work, not transactional projects. Quarterly architecture review. Documented monthly Aufwandsbericht.
