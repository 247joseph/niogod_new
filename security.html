The legal architecture under every niogod™ deployment. Three contractually anchored protective layers: AÜG-Geltungsbereich, Werkvertrag-Architektur, IP/GDPR-Souveränität.

Why DACH compliance is not optional.

// 02

The three layers.

// Layer 1 · BGB §631 ff.

Werkvertrag-Architektur.

niogod™ delivers a defined Werk (the pod's deliverables, with acceptance/Abnahme criteria), not Dienste. This places the engagement on the BGB §631 side of the line, not §611 (Dienstvertrag) and not §1 AÜG (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung). The Senior Consultant and Pilot Engineer take direction exclusively from the Projektleiter — the Mandant cannot issue task-level Weisungen to offshore engineers, which is the legal trip-wire for verdeckte AÜG.

// Layer 2 · BA-Weisungen 2024 / 2025

AÜG-Geltungsbereich.

As of the Fachlichen Weisungen der Bundesagentur für Arbeit (15. Oktober 2024, revised 1. Oktober 2025), engineers who remain physically outside Germany and operate online for an Entleiher in Germany do not trigger the Erlaubnisvorbehalt under §1 AÜG — on the condition that no onsite Einsatz in Germany takes place. niogod™'s Einsatz-Protocol enforces this strictly: any required onsite presence is executed by the Stuttgart-based Projektleiter, never by the offshore Pilot Engineer.

// Layer 3 · Art. 28 DSGVO

IP-Souveränität & GDPR.

Work-for-hire clauses assigning all created IP to the Mandant's GmbH/UG/AG immediately upon creation. Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag (AVV) under Art. 28 DSGVO. AWS Frankfurt as default processing region. ISO 27001 — implementation in progress, target Q3 2026. Signed NDA enforceable in DE jurisdiction (Gerichtsstand Stuttgart). Optional BSI C5 / TISAX alignment available for medtech and defense Mandanten.

// 05

Counsel-ready answers.

Is niogod™ an EOR?

No. EOR is a payroll product. niogod™ is an engineering delivery product structured as a Werkvertrag with deliverables and acceptance criteria. niogod™'s Indian operating entity (Borcom Dynamics Pvt Ltd) is the employer of the engineers; the Mandant has no employment relationship with them.

What about onsite Einsätze in Germany?

Brief onsite presence is coordinated exclusively through the Stuttgart-anchored Projektleiter. Permanent or extended onsite work by the Pilot Engineer is explicitly out of scope to preserve the AÜG posture.

Where is data processed?

AWS Frankfurt (eu-central-1) by default. Bring-Your-Own-Cloud option available. AVV per Art. 28 DSGVO signed before kickoff. Standard Contractual Clauses for any cross-border transfer fully disclosed.

Will our Steuerberater approve this?

A 30-minute counsel-to-counsel call is offered before signature. The Mustervertrag, AÜG-Whitepaper, and AVV-Template are downloadable. niogod™ is advised by a deutsche Fachanwaltskanzlei für Arbeits- und IT-Recht.

What jurisdiction governs the contract?

Verträge unter deutschem Recht, Gerichtsstand Stuttgart. The DACH Liaison is anchored at Pfaffenwaldring 50, 70569 Stuttgart.
